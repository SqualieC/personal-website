Hi, I'm Squalie. I'm a math major with a background in physics and computer science, and I play bass — most of the projects on this site started as "I wish this tool existed" while working on something else.

## What I make

I write small, focused tools rather than big platforms: a live pitch-detection practice app for bass and guitar, a Korean reading practice app with neural text-to-speech, an Overwatch stats tracker that reads its own screenshots, and a few browser games and utilities. Everything here is built, documented, and shipped end to end — usually as an Astro site, a Python desktop app, or a small Cloudflare Worker.

## Outside of code

When I'm not building things, I'm usually playing bass, listening to something new (see the homepage for what's currently on repeat), or out taking photos — you can find a growing collection of those in the [photo sets](/photos).
